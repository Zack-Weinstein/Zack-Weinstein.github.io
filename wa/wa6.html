Typography

Space Grotesk for headings and body, JetBrains Mono for code. Sizes use a 1.25 scale.

Heading 1 - 2.441rem

Heading 2 - 1.953rem

Heading 3 - 1.563rem

Body text - 1rem with a 1.6 line height.

Small text - 0.8rem, for captions and footnotes.

--color-primary: #9C3A22;

Spacing System

8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px. Used for all margins, padding, and gaps on this page.
